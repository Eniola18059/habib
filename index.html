Who We Are

A community built to bridge the gap

WILLNET was created to give women practical opportunities to grow their leadership skills while building meaningful connections with other women on similar journeys.

Too often, women are told to "be confident" or "step up," yet they are rarely given the tools, mentorship, or environments needed to actually develop those capabilities.

At WILLNET, leadership is not about titles — it is about influence, growth, and impact. We believe it can be learned, practiced, and developed in community.

Meet the Founder

The vision behind WILLNET

WILLNET was founded by Aisha Umar, a leader, strategist, and community builder deeply committed to empowering women and supporting leadership development.

With more than a decade of experience across nonprofit, business, and community sectors, Aisha observed a consistent pattern — talented women holding back, not from lack of ambition, but from lack of the right infrastructure to support their growth.

"Leadership is not limited to titles — it is about influence, growth, and the courage to step forward."
